METHOD · A DOUBLE OPERAD
Atoms are the generators. An operator takes n units and returns one — a unit is a tree, and every ancestor re-derives when a leaf moves. Colour rides alongside as a rank into the palette; where two forms overlap the ranks glue to a third, so nothing off-palette can sound. Associate binds forms two at a time — its map of bracketings is the associahedron.
