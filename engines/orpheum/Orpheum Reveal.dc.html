WHAT CHANGES IT
Veil α and blend. Multiply compounds: every stacked veil keeps only part of the tone beneath. Lower α, or set blend to normal. Try the Blend override tweak here first.
Where the Veil sits in the tree. A Veil inside an echo or harmonic is copied with every echo. Put it above the operator so it paints once.
Depth. Fewer layers, lower shadow opacity, or shade ‘rank’ instead of ink.
Glue to ink. Overlaps resolve to (a+b+1) mod m; when that lands on the darkest swatch the overlap goes black. Shift a rank, or turn recut off.
