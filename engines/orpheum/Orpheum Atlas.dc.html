γ · THE OPERADn in, one out

An operation takes n units and returns one

Plugging g into an input of f is the composition γ. A unit is a tree; evaluating from the leaves up is the algebra. Move a leaf and every ancestor re-derives — and because an unchanged subtree returns the same value, only the branch you touched is recomputed. Flatten forgets the tree and keeps its value; Ungroup peels one level.

∑ eⁱᵏᶿ · THE FOURIER RECUTtruncation = abstraction

A form is the sum of its partials

Trace the outline as a periodic signal, keep K terms, resynthesise. Few terms: the square forgets its corners and becomes a disc with a memory. Which terms you keep is a ladder — all integers, odd only (the square-wave ladder), Fibonacci, or φ: 1·2·4·7·11·18, which never closes on itself. This is the dial from likeness to primary.

137.5° · THE φ ECHO360° / φ²

The form returns smaller, turned, never repeating

echoes · about {{echoAbout}}{{eN}}

Each echo is scaled by 1/φ and turned by the golden angle. Because 137.5° is the most irrational turn there is, successive copies never align — the same reason seeds pack evenly on a sunflower (right). About self makes the form its own centre; about plate makes the plate the centre.

Scale, turn, step — applied again to the result each time, so the copies trace a spiral, a fan, or a ladder depending on the ratios. Where the harmonic series divides, the affine series multiplies. Each step shifts rank by one.

Bind n forms two at a time with a law that is not associative — (ab)c and a(bc) differ — and the bracketings become points. Each vertex is a full bracketing; each edge is one re-association. For four forms the map is a pentagon; for five, a solid with fourteen vertices. The slider moves along an edge: a straight-line path between two bracketings, every frame a valid plate.

⊗ ℱ · THE DOUBLE, AND THE SHEAF(shape, rank, mode)

Colour belongs to the field, not the form

SOLID ∧ SOLID → GLUE
{{gluEq}}
VEIL OVER SOLID → BLEND
α 0.55 · multiply

A form carries a rank, and every geometric operation carries a rank action — two operads acting on the pair (shape, rank). That is the double. Where two solids meet, the intersection is re-cut to glue(a,b) = (a+b+1) mod m — symmetric, so draw order cannot matter and nothing off-palette can appear. That is the sheaf: colour is a section over the plate, decided by what is stacked at each point. A veil is the one declared exception: a translucent layer above every solid, never re-cut.

What is beneath shows through, un-recut

The same three forms. Left, all solid: each overlap is re-cut to a palette member. Right, the triangle is a veil: it floats above every solid and tints what it crosses. Multiply darkens like a glaze, screen lightens like a projection, normal simply fades. Palette closure is relaxed on veils by declaration — they are a different section, the field's luminance.

▱ · DEPTHflat colour, stacked

A third dimension without a rendered light

light from °{{dAngle}}

Depth is a unit offset along a light angle in flat layers. Ink shade is a translucent tone chosen by the ground's luminance — dark multiplied on a light plate, light screened on a dark one — fading with distance; ground is the same tone at a constant opacity; rank shade is a solid extrusion stepping through the palette — the strict option, since every fill stays a palette member. A plate-wide relief in Tweaks does the same for every solid at once.

⇪ · INGEST AND DECOMPOSITIONsource → generators

A drawing enters as parts, not as a picture

Upload a drawing or photograph and it is traced into polygons by tone band, each band a group, the whole under one Ingest unit whose sliders re-trace live. Then decompose: Convexity split cuts each form at its hollows into near-convex parts — limbs named without a name; Fourier split keeps a low-K solid body and lays the full form over it as a veil, so a likeness becomes a tint on a primary. From there every operator applies.

The unit is rasterised, transformed, its spectrum reshaped — a slope tilts energy between coarse and fine, a sector comb keeps only N-fold symmetric frequencies — then inverted, thresholded into tone levels, and traced back to closed polygons. The result is vector again and takes every other operator. It is the one place geometry and the k-space lab are the same instrument.

Geometry that has passed through its own spectrum

The 2-D Fourier transform of the whole plate. Its radial power falls as 1/fβ; β is a texture number and D the matching fractal dimension. We measure it to keep plates in a legible band — and use it as an operator: rasterise a unit, filter its spectrum (slope, sector combs, φ-rings, echoes), trace the result back to vectors. The k-space return is the one operation that leaves vector space and comes back.

TRANSCENDENTAL REALISM · WHY THESE SYMBOLSafter Adi Da Samraj

The image is not a picture of something. It is a condition the viewer enters.

Adi Da named his late image-art Transcendental Realism and described its working principles as Geome — primary geometric form, replicated and composed — and Linead, the free line as its balancing pole. His words for what the images are: aperspectival, anegoic, aniconic. The mathematics here is chosen because it describes what such plates already do, not to decorate them. Orpheum generates its own plates from rules; it reproduces none of his.

γ → NO PART STANDS ALONE
Every form is the output of a composition. There is no atom on the plate that is not already a relation.
ℱ → COLOUR BELONGS TO THE WHOLE
A form does not own its colour; the field decides it where forms meet. The palette is a law, not a choice made shape by shape.
Kₙ → THE POINT OF VIEW IS NOT FIXED
The same elements, every way of binding them, held as one space. A single plate is one vertex; the suite is the polytope. Aperspectival, literally.
∑ → ABSTRACTION IS TRUNCATION
Dropping partials is how a form moves from likeness to primary. Aniconic by construction: the dial from faithful to radical is how many terms you keep.
RULES → ANEGOIC
Forms are placed by ratio and the composition is edited as a tree. The hand chooses the law; the law places the form. The gesture is not the subject.
900 DPI → MONUMENTAL
Vector first, printed at archival resolution in six formats up to 21:9. The plate is meant to be stood in front of.
USES · WHAT THE STRUCTURE MAKES POSSIBLE
Editions. Walk every vertex of Kn and print the suite — one composition, fourteen plates, each a different bracketing.
Motion. An edge walk is a sequence of valid plates; a path around the pentagon is a loop. Frames come free.
Spectral return. Raster the plate, filter in k-space, trace back to vectors: geometry that has passed through its own spectrum.
From life. Ingest a drawing, split it into parts, and let the parts take the operators — a figure becomes a chord of primaries.
Sound. Ranks are already indices; the harmonic operator is already an overtone row. A plate can be played as it is drawn.
Teaching. Catalan numbers, Fourier truncation, sheaf axioms — each shown by a form you can drag, not a formula you must trust.
Portability. The tree saves as one .orpheum.json — structure, palette, sources — and reopens as it was.
Open on the ledger. True triple gluing, workers for the heavy ops, transform-only drag — see the review ledger.
