The Architecture

Stop hand-drawing coordinates. Compile visuals from a grounded spec.

Instead of hand-baking coordinates and hex codes on every prompt, the foundry binds every visual mark to a verbatim transcript span, solves layout deterministically with real Chromium font metrics, layers 2.5D volumetric primitives across 4 depth planes, and verifies every pixel against an 8-gate harness (`H1–H8`).

1 · Ground

Evidence spans

Source adapters (Wispr, Krisp, briefs, course outlines) cut the material into short verbatim spans with stable ids. Agents work from the span index, not the raw transcript.

CoDA schema and statistics, never raw rows
2 · Extract

Concepts, relations, claims

Actors, objects, quantities, outcomes and tensions, each citing spans. Claims carry a rhetoric: mechanism, choice, derivation, tension, union.

VIS-GEN intent model · −14.6 pp without it
3 · Propose

Three idioms per claim

Structurally different metaphors, scored on fidelity, clarity, novelty, feasibility. Losers keep a written reason. This is where Milton's variants table lives.

VIS-GEN 4 candidates + rubric · −5.8 pp with one
4 · Compose

Layout tree with ports

Boxes and flex weights only. A solver places everything with real font metrics; parents own every arrow that crosses a boundary.

FigTree −2.11 without recursion, −1.35 without parent-owned edges
5 · Draw (2.5D)

Volumetric glyph programs

Leaves call parameterised 4-plane glyphs (`data-z="0..3"`, `data-seq="0..k"`): plinths, axonometric blocks, calipers, sculpted actors. Role tokens only, zero raw hex.

FigTree + Volumetric Pen constrained SVG vocabulary
6 · Gate (H1–H8)

Deterministic harness

Refs, provenance of every printed number, vocabulary, Chromium-measured collisions & spills, WCAG contrast, complexity floor, beat coverage, and Z-layer depth verification.

MultiVis-Agent rule layer · +25 pp task success
7 · Critique

Render-critic, anchored

A fresh agent sees the 2× PNG, the spec and the source; returns typed fixes pinned to node ids. Local repair, gated by the same provenance check.

FigTree · SciFig · DataReel 3 rounds is where gains flatten
8 · Skin

Style record, injected last

Palette, volumetric shadow/rim tokens, type roles, stroke widths and hand-wobble live in one record. Swapping it re-skins the entire film instantaneously.

PaperBanana stylist re-gated by H5 contrast
9 · Direct

Camera & particle theatre

Spring-damped viewBox camera framing, 2.5D perspective parallax, Bezier edge flow particles, WebAudio synthesis, and live parametric DOM re-compilation.

DataMagic · DataReel narration-indexed choreography
The Spec & Verification Gates

SymSpec & H1–H8 Deterministic Harness

Every span is verbatim; concepts and relations cite spans; claims carry rhetoric; candidates record why alternatives were rejected; layout leaves cite concepts; beats trigger by narration index.

__SCHEMA__

8 Binary Harness Checks (0 Errors · 0 Warnings)

  • H1 refs — every id resolves; every leaf cites a concept, every edge cites a relation
  • H2 provenance — quotes verbatim in source; every printed or spoken number grounded in cited spans, number words included
  • H3 vocabulary — allow-listed tags only; zero <filter>, <image>, or raw hex outside the <style> block
  • H4 geometry — text-text collisions, edge-through-text, canvas bounds, and leaf box spills measured in headless Chromium with real @fontsource fonts
  • H5 contrast — WCAG contrast ratio $\ge 4.5:1$ per text role across all 3 style records (`milton-warm`, `deepmind-iridescent`, `ceti-night`)
  • H6 complexity floor — balanced-tree minimum drawn elements per glyph so nothing gets “fixed” by deleting content
  • H7 beats — every semantic claim (`k0–k5`) is revealed by at least one choreographed beat
  • H8 depth layering — every volumetric glyph encodes $\ge 3$ distinct `data-z` planes (`Z0` plinths $\rightarrow$ `Z1` mechanisms $\rightarrow$ `Z2` actors $\rightarrow$ `Z3` callouts)

Why gating the critic matters

Key Verification Insight

Vision-language critics hallucinate too. During iteration, the critic twice proposed labels the transcript never supports (“mo 2 · 3 d/wk”, “12 months +”, “after · on call”). Because the deterministic provenance gate (`H2`) runs on every critic repair—not just the initial generator draft—all ungrounded critic suggestions were automatically rejected.

Similarly, Wispr Flow diarization labeled every turn “Victoria”, so the spec marks speaker attribution unreliable and visually frames the 8 : 2–4 ratio as an unattributed structural counterpoint.
